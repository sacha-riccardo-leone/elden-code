    The Lands Between Sessions

    This is a public demo. Every Tarnished here is a made-up Claude Code session: projects rise and sink, sessions come and go, and nothing is real or sent anywhere. Run Elden Code on your own machine to see your own sessions.

    Each Claude Code session on this machine walks here as a Tarnished. Every project has its own region, an island with its own biome, joined to Roundtable Hold in the middle by stone bridges. Cloth carries the project's colour; plume, eyes or feather carry the model's.

    • The Forge — editing and writing files
    • The Scriptorium — reading and searching
    • The Ritual Circle — shell commands
    • The Raven Tower — web and MCP tools
    • The War Table — plans and todo lists
    • The Fog Gate — kneeling for thy permission; departing sessions pass through it
    • Spirit ashes — subagents, hovering beside their summoner
    • Site of Grace — rest after a quest; idle Tarnished wander and talk
    • Roundtable Hold — idle Tarnished from every project come to sit at the round table and talk

    Thou art Maren the Stormbound, or anyone thou choosest to become: W A S D or the arrow keys to walk (hold Shift to run), and cross the bridges to other islands. Left click to strike, Space to dodge, C to crouch, B to open thy fan of gestures (or 1–8; none while a great enemy is abroad); nearby Tarnished may answer. E uses what thou standest by: the forge, the scrolls, the war table, the ritual circle, the raven tower, the fog gate, a site of grace, and the Round Table (which can summon a great enemy to the Arena of Trials, north of the Hold; great enemies also come by themselves, and when a session struggles; as one comes thou choosest up to four companions to fight beside thee, the mages from afar, the rest up close, and Start fight raises the arena; right click casts a spell as Fortuna or a mage, and once thy bar is full R looses thine ultimate). M shows the map. Z / X travel by grace to the previous or next area, H to Roundtable Hold. Click another Tarnished to inspect it; the fan over its head lets thee become them. Maren lives her own life meanwhile; click her and become her again to return. Drag a Tarnished to carry it, scroll to zoom. Click a line in the log to travel to whoever it's about. P shows frame rate and resolution.

    Day and night take turns, 18 minutes each, the last 2 a slow dusk or dawn. Add ?time=day or ?time=night to the address to hold one, or ?time=fast to watch a whole day pass in two minutes.

    Read-only: nothing here can send anything back to Claude Code.
